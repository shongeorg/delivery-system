# Finances

`GET /owner/finances` calculates:

```
revenue = sum of totalPrice from orders where status != 'cancelled' AND paymentStatus = 'paid'
netProfit = revenue - payroll(period) - expenses(period)
```

Cash orders with `paymentStatus: pending` do not count toward revenue until marked `paid` (no endpoint for this).
